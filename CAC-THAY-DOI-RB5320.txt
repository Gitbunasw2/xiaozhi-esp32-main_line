2) BOARD MOI
   a. Bread Compact WiFi Multi (cau hinh chan qua web 192.168.4.1)
      main/boards/bread-compact-wifi-multi/
      - Chan GPIO doc tu NVS (rb5320/device_json), cau hinh o tab "Cau hinh Thiet bi"
      - Man TFT SPI: ST7789/P3, ST7735/S, ILI9341/9342C, ILI9486, ILI9488, ILI9163C,
        ST7796/S, GC9A01, GC9107/9106, NV3023. OLED I2C: SSD1306, SH1106
      - Cau hinh theo dinh dang config.json (Xuat/Nhap/Luu cung 1 dinh dang, mau: pin-config-mau.json)
      - Am thanh I2S Simplex / Duplex / chip ES8311, LED WS2812, den phu, pin + bao sac
      - Robot RB5320 (motor, HC-SR04, 2 LED, lenh giong noi) cau hinh tu web, mach mau "Robot RB5320"
      - The nho microSD + phat nhac MP3 bang giong noi, man OLED phu (cau hinh tu web)
      - Chon driver motor: 4 chan, TB6612, L298N, L293D, DRV8833, L9110S, MX1508, BTS7960
      - Chuc nang robot bat/tat: Tranh vat can, Do line PID 6 cam bien (o chan mo khi tat)
      - Trang 192.168.4.1: bam Luu tu nhay toi chan trung/sai, chan cam 0/39/40/35/36/37,
        ghi chu TX/RX 43/44 (thao day khi nap)
      - Chuc nang "Dieu khien Bluetooth AT-09 (app Dabble)"; the nho, OLED phu, ESP32-WROOM A2DP
        deu co o tick, khong dung thi mo di va khong tinh trung chan
      - Logo RB5320XZ78AI luc khoi dong tu thu phong dung ti le cho moi kich thuoc man (ban goc net 772x700)
      - Hinh nen sang/toi tu gian vua khit moi kich thuoc man LCD, giu tron anh, khong cat,
        lam net + dither; nap firmware moi tu ve giao dien SANG
      - Nut CO DINH: BOOT = GPIO0, Vol+ = GPIO40, Vol- = GPIO39 (khong doi tu web)
      - Kiem tra chan sai/trung, che do an toan khi crash 3 lan
      - Xem HUONG-DAN.txt trong thu muc board
   b. Nologo Xingzhi Cube 4.0-inch TFT ILI9488 Wi-Fi
      main/boards/nologo/xingzhi-cube-4.0tft-ili9488-wifi/  (320x480, SPI 18-bit)
   c. Nologo Xingzhi Cube 2.8-inch TFT ST7789 Wi-Fi
      main/boards/nologo/xingzhi-cube-2.8tft-st7789-wifi/
   d. Nologo Xingzhi Cube 2.8-inch TFT ILI9341 Wi-Fi
      main/boards/nologo/xingzhi-cube-2.8tft-ili9341-wifi/
   e. RB HC04 + man phu (truoc day ten "RB5320 Robot"; thu muc van la rb5320)  (logo khoi dong lam lai cho net)
      main/boards/rb5320/  - logo RB5320 320x240 khi khoi dong
      CAP NHAT: bo hong ngoai 41/42; them the nho microSD (MISO 42, CLK 41, MOSI 20, CS 19)
      phat nhac MP3 bang giong noi; them man OLED phu SSD1306 0.96" (SDA 3, SCL 46) hien gio,
      Wi-Fi, pin, mat robot theo cam xuc, khoang cach HC-SR04, chu AI dang noi.
      Code robot dung chung voi board Multi (bread-compact-wifi-multi/rb_robot.h).


   f. RB Do Line PID (ST7789 2.8", TB6612 6 chan, cam bien line 6 kenh)
      main/boards/robot-line-pid/  - TB6612: AIN1 21, AIN2 47, PWMA 12, BIN1 20, BIN2 19, PWMB 11
      Cam bien line S0..S5: 1, 41, 2, 48, 42, 3. PID dieu chinh bang giong noi, hieu chinh cam bien.
      Giu Vol+: bat/tat do line, giu Vol-: hieu chinh cam bien.
   g. RB AT09 + HC04  (main/boards/rb-at09-hc04/, dung chung code rb5320)
      Bo OLED phu, Bluetooth AT-09: ESP TX 46 -> RXD, ESP RX 3 <- TXD. App Dabble GamePad:
      START = tranh vat can, SELECT = lai tay, mui ten/joystick lai, X dung, VUONG den.
   h. RB Do Line PID + AT09  (main/boards/robot-line-pid-at09/, dung chung code robot-line-pid)
      Bluetooth AT-09: ESP TX 46, ESP RX 38. START = do line, SELECT = lai tay, TRON = hieu chinh.

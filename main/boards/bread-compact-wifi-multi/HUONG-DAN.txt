BOARD DA NANG: bread-compact-wifi-multi (ESP32-S3, Wi-Fi)
=========================================================
Mot firmware dung cho nhieu mach. Chan GPIO KHONG nam trong config.h ma duoc
cau hinh tren trang Wi-Fi http://192.168.4.1 -> tab "Cau hinh Thiet bi".
Doi chan / doi man hinh chi can Luu + Khoi dong lai, KHONG can build lai.

1) BUILD (1 lan)
   idf.py set-target esp32s3
   idf.py menuconfig
     Xiaozhi Assistant -> Board Type -> Bread Compact WiFi Multi (cau hinh chan qua web 192.168.4.1)
   idf.py build
   idf.py merge-bin   -> build/merged-binary.bin

   Project phai co folder components/78__esp-wifi-connect (ban da sua) vi trang
   cau hinh va API /rb/device nam trong do.

2) CAU HINH CHAN
   - Chua luu Wi-Fi: board tu phat Wi-Fi "RB5320XZ78AI-xxxx".
     Da luu Wi-Fi: bam nut BOOT trong luc dang khoi dong (hoac xoa Wi-Fi da luu)
     de vao che do cau hinh.
   - Ket noi dien thoai vao Wi-Fi do, mo http://192.168.4.1
   - Tab "Cau hinh Thiet bi": chon mach co san hoac nhap tay -> "Luu cau hinh"
     -> "Khoi dong lai".
   - Mo lai trang: o mau xanh/vang o dau tab cho biet board dang chay gi va
     cac canh bao (trung chan, chan sai...).

3) HO TRO
   Man hinh : khong man, hoac TFT SPI:
                ST7789/ST7789V/P3, ST7735/ST7735S, ILI9341/ILI9342C, ILI9486,
                ILI9488 (18-bit), ILI9163C, ST7796/ST7796S, GC9A01, GC9107/GC9106, NV3023
              OLED I2C (dia chi 0x3C): SSD1306, SH1106 - 128x32 hoac 128x64
              * ST7735, ILI9486, ILI9163C, GC9107 dung chuoi khoi tao rieng tren driver
                chung -> neu mau sai/am ban: doi RGB<->BGR, Dao mau; lech hinh: chinh Offset
              * ILI9486 loai man "Raspberry Pi 3.5inch" (co IC dich 16-bit) co the khong chay
   Am thanh : I2S Simplex (INMP441 + MAX98357, 6 chan), Duplex (4 chan),
              hoac chip ES8311 (MCLK, BCLK, LRCK, DOUT, DIN + I2C SDA/SCL, PA loa)
              ES8311 dung chung bus I2C voi OLED neu cung chan SDA/SCL
   Logo     : RB5320XZ78AI hien luc khoi dong tren MOI man (TFT va OLED), tu thu phong
              giu dung ti le theo Rong x Cao da cau hinh (khong meo), can giua nen den.
              Man tron GC9A01 de le rong hon de logo nam trong vong tron.
              Tu an khi ket noi Wi-Fi hoac vao che do cau hinh.
              Logo goc: rb_logo_1bpp.c - ban do phan giai cao 772x700 (1-bit), firmware luon
              THU NHO (trung binh dien tich) -> net, vien muot o moi kich thuoc
   Hinh nen : sang + toi (chi man LCD), tu GIAN VUA KHIT man, giu TRON anh, KHONG cat
              (co the hoi meo), KHONG xoay: anh luon dung doc ke ca man nam ngang. Bat/tat bang o "Hinh nen sang/toi" (wallpaper 0/1).
              Can PSRAM (~2 x Rong x Cao x 2 byte); khong du thi tu bo qua.
              Anh goc: rb_wallpaper.c (240x320, mau 24-bit). Phong to bicubic + lam net,
              thu nho trung binh dien tich, dither khi ve 16-bit. SANG = anh mach den,
              TOI = anh mach vang. Moi lan NAP FIRMWARE MOI tu ve giao dien SANG
              (doi sang toi sau do van duoc giu cho toi lan nap tiep theo).
              Muon sieu net tren man lon: can anh goc lon hon (>= 480x640).
   Chan man : SDA / MOSI (DISPLAY_SDA), SCL / CLK (DISPLAY_SCL), DC / RS (DISPLAY_DC),
              CS (DISPLAY_CS), RES / RST (DISPLAY_RES), BL / LED (DISPLAY_BACKLIGHT_PIN)
   Robot    : chon DRIVER motor: 4 chan (nhu RB5320), TB6612FNG (6 chan + STBY), L298N (+ENA/ENB),
              L293D (+EN1/EN2), DRV8833, L9110S, MX1508 (PWM tren chan IN), BTS7960/IBT-2.
              Ten o chan tu doi theo driver (BIN1/AIN1/PWMA..., IN1/ENA..., RPWM/LPWM...).
              Dao chieu motor trai/phai bang o tick (khong can dao day).
              CHUC NANG: tick "Tranh vat can" (HC-SR04) va/hoac "Do line PID" (6 cam bien) moi mo
              o chan cua chuc nang do; chuc nang tat -> o mo di, chan bi bo qua, khong tinh trung.
              Do line: S0 = trai nhat. GPIO1-10 doc analog, 11-20 (ADC2) kem on dinh khi bat Wi-Fi,
              chan khac doc digital 0/1. Kp/Ki/Kd, toc do, kieu vach (den/trang).
              Lenh: "do line", "dung do line", "hieu chinh cam bien", "chinh PID kp 0.05 kd 0.2".
              Bluetooth AT-09 / HM-10 (app Dabble GamePad): ESP TX -> RXD, ESP RX <- TXD (RX khong
              duoc la GPIO46). START = tranh vat can (hoac do line neu chi bat do line),
              SELECT = ve lai tay, mui ten/joystick lai, X dung, VUONG den, TRON hieu chinh line /
              lac lu, TAM GIAC do line (khi bat ca 2 chuc nang) / ca giut.
              toc do motor % (noi "chay cham lai / nhanh len" de doi), HC-SR04 TRIG/ECHO, LED 1, LED 2,
              khoang cach ne vat can (cm), thoi gian quay 360 do (ms), tu quay/lac khi ranh.
              Lenh giong noi: tien, lui, re trai/phai, dung, tu tranh vat can, bat/tat den,
              do khoang cach, lac lu, ca giut, the hien bat ngo. Chon mach mau "Robot RB5320".
   The nho  : microSD qua SPI (MISO, CLK, MOSI, CS) -> /sdcard. The FAT32, file .mp3 de o thu muc
              goc hoac thu muc "music" / "nhac", TEN FILE KHONG DAU (vd: Lac Troi - Son Tung.mp3).
              Muon ten CO DAU: menuconfig -> Component config -> FAT Filesystem support ->
              API character encoding -> UTF-8 (build bang scripts/build.py thi da tu bat).
              Lenh giong noi: "mo nhac", "mo bai Lac Troi", "mo bai so 3", "bai tiep", "bai truoc",
              "tiep tuc nghe nhac", "tat nhac", "co bai nao". Nhac phat khi robot RANH; bam nut BOOT
              hoac goi robot thi nhac dung (noi "tiep tuc nghe nhac" de phat lai bai do).
              MP3 moi tan so mau (44.1/48 kHz...), stereo tu tron thanh mono.
   OLED phu : SSD1306/SH1106 I2C 0x3C (128x64 hoac 128x32), chay song song voi man chinh:
              gio | trang thai | Wi-Fi | pin ; 2 mat robot theo cam xuc (chop mat, liec mat,
              vui/buon/gian/ngac nhien/yeu/ngu/nhay mat/suy nghi/ngau/boi roi/khoc) ;
              khoang cach HC-SR04 ; chu AI dang noi (bo dau, tu cuon dong).
              Dung bus I2C rieng; neu trung SDA/SCL voi ES8311 thi dung chung bus ES8311.
              OLED chinh + ES8311 bus rieng da chiem het 2 bus I2C -> khong them OLED phu duoc.
   Luu y chan: GPIO19/20 = USB D-/D+ (dung thi mat cong USB native, nap qua cong UART).
              GPIO46 = chan strapping: OLED/I2C keo len muc 1 -> khi nap firmware o che do
              download co the bao loi -> rut OLED (hoac day VCC OLED) ra roi nap.
   Nut      : CO DINH, khong doi tu web: BOOT/Wake = GPIO0, Tang am = GPIO40, Giam am = GPIO39
              Thiet bi khac dat trung 0/39/40 se bi bo (hoac tat man / dung am thanh mac dinh)
   LED      : WS2812 (1 bong hoac dai nhieu bong)
   Den phu  : chan bat/tat den/relay, AI dieu khien bang giong noi
   Pin      : chan ADC GPIO1-20, cau chia ap 100k/100k (sua BATTERY_DIVIDER_* trong config.h)
              Bao sac: "Active HIGH" tat = LOW khi sac (TP4056/BQ24, tu bat pull-up)
   Chua dung: Bluetooth A2DP, Ngon ngu (chon luc build), Giao dien WeChat, Do nhay mic

4) FILE CAU HINH config.json (nut "Xuat / Nhap config.json" tren trang)
   Mau: pin-config-mau.json trong thu muc nay. Firmware luu dung dinh dang nay.
   screen_type           0 = TFT LCD SPI, 1 = OLED I2C, 2 = khong man hinh
   oled_driver           0 = SSD1306, 1 = SH1106
   driver (TFT)          0 ST7789, 1 ST7735, 2 ILI9341, 3 GC9A01, 4 ILI9488,
                         5 ST7796, 6 ILI9486, 7 ILI9163C, 8 GC9107, 9 NV3023
   rgb_order             0 = RGB, 1 = BGR
   audio_codec_type      0 = I2S Simplex, 1 = I2S Duplex, 2 = ES8311
   charging_active_level 0 = LOW khi sac, 1 = HIGH khi sac
   language              0 = vi-VN, 1 = en-US (chi luu; ngon ngu chon luc build)
   mirror_x/y, swap_xy, invert_color, backlight_invert: 0/1
   pin_* , i2s_* , codec_* : so GPIO, -1 = khong gan
   pin_boot / pin_vol_up / pin_vol_dn: luon 0 / 40 / 39 (bo qua gia tri trong file)
   Them (khong bat buoc): spi_clock_mhz (0 = tu dong), codec_pa_inverted (0/1), wallpaper (0/1)
   Robot: robot_enable (0/1), motor_driver (0-7), pin_motor_l1, pin_motor_l2, pin_motor_r1, pin_motor_r2,
          pin_hc04_trig, pin_hc04_echo, pin_led1, pin_led2,
          obstacle_cm (25), spin_360_ms (2200), robot_idle_move (0/1),
          pin_motor_pwm_l, pin_motor_pwm_r (EN/PWM), pin_motor_stby, motor_speed (20-100),
          motor_invert_l (mac dinh 1), motor_invert_r (mac dinh 0)
          func_avoid (0/1), func_line (0/1), pin_line_0..pin_line_5, line_kp, line_ki, line_kd,
          line_speed (%), line_white (0 vach den, 1 vach trang); sd_enable, oled2_enable (0/1)
          func_bt (0/1), pin_at09_tx, pin_at09_rx, at09_baud (9600); bt_a2dp_enable (0/1)
   The nho: pin_sd_miso, pin_sd_clk, pin_sd_mosi, pin_sd_cs   (-1 = khong co the)
   OLED phu: pin_oled2_sda, pin_oled2_scl, oled2_driver (0 SSD1306, 1 SH1106),
             oled2_height (64 / 32), oled2_flip (0/1 lat 180 do)
   Mau RB5320 day du (man + robot + the nho + OLED phu): pin-config-rb5320.json
   Mau RB Do Line PID (TB6612 + 6 cam bien):              pin-config-line-pid.json
   Mau RB AT09 + HC04 / RB Do Line PID + AT09:            pin-config-rb-at09.json, pin-config-line-pid-at09.json
   Chua dung: use_wechat, rotate_screen, pin_bluetooth_uart_tx/rx, pin_dout2

5) TU BAO VE
   - Bam LUU tren trang: tu kiem tra, nhay toi o bi trung / sai (vien do) va bao doi chan.
     Chan cam: 0, 39, 40 (nut), 35, 36, 37 (PSRAM), 22-34, RST/EN.
     GPIO43 (TX) / 44 (RX): dung duoc nhung LUC NAP CODE PHAI THAO DAY 2 chan nay ra.
   - Chan khong ton tai, chan flash/PSRAM (26-37 voi module N8R8/N16R8) -> bo qua
   - Trung chan -> bo thiet bi uu tien thap hon. Thu tu uu tien: nut co dinh > am thanh >
     man hinh > robot > the nho > OLED phu > LED/den/pin/HC-SR04/LED robot
   - Man khoi tao loi -> chay khong man hinh, van noi chuyen duoc
   - Board crash lien tiep 3 lan (panic/watchdog/sut ap) -> CHE DO AN TOAN:
     tat man hinh + ngoai vi, dung am thanh mac dinh (4,5,6,7,15,16), tu mo
     trang 192.168.4.1 de sua. Chay on dinh 20 giay thi bo dem tu xoa.

6) CHAN MAC DINH (khi chua cau hinh)
   Mic: WS 4, SCK 5, DIN 6 | Loa: DOUT 7, BCLK 15, LRCK 16 | Nut: BOOT 0, Vol+ 40, Vol- 39 | Khong man hinh
